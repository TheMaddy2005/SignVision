PRIVACY POLICY

SignVision — Last updated November 2026

1. Introduction

SignVision ("we", "our", "us") operates the website https://sign-vision-tau.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the service handles when you use it. By using SignVision, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Handle

SignVision does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

  • Text you type: Sent to the SignVision API only to look up the matching sign images.
  • Speech: Converted to text in your browser by its built-in speech recognition; only the resulting text is sent for the sign lookup.

3. How We Use It

Your input is used exclusively to return the matching sign images and animations. Inputs are not stored, logged, shared, or used to build profiles. We do not sell, rent, or monetize any data.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Vercel — hosting and serverless API; may process standard technical logs (https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Your browser vendor — speech recognition is a browser feature and may be processed by your browser's speech service

5. Data Retention

Your text and speech inputs are processed in memory to produce the translation and are not retained afterwards.

6. Children's Privacy

SignVision does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of SignVision after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
